# Swishy AI

## Why Use It

Producing animated product explainers and kinetic data visualizations typically demands deep expertise in complex motion software like After Effects or Remotion. Swishy AI functions as an automated generative motion designer that translates text scripts and raw data into sleek, choreographed UI animations and explainer videos. It automates keyframing, typography staging, and camera transitions through natural language instructions.

## Key Capabilities

- Text-to-motion engine generating kinetic typography, UI mockups, and smooth camera pans
- Automated data visualization animations transforming raw numbers and metrics into dynamic motion charts
- Timeline and styling customization allowing real-time adjustment of easing curves, colors, and asset timing
- Export pipelines supporting high-frame-rate MP4 video, transparent WebM, and lightweight animated GIFs

## Stack Fit & Workflow

Pairs with developer marketing, documentation portals, and SaaS landing pages built with React, Next.js, or standard video publishing workflows.
