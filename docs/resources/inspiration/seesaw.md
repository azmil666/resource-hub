# SEESAW

## Why Use It

Mainstream web galleries often prioritize conventional corporate layouts, neglecting experimental typography, unconventional layouts, and innovative frontend engineering. SEESAW is an independent design gallery that curates avant-garde, highly interactive websites and creative digital experiences from global design studios and independent developers. It serves as a visual playground for engineers looking to break outside generic design molds.

## Key Capabilities

- Curated showcase of experimental, boundary-pushing web design and interactive digital experiences
- Emphasis on unconventional typographic layouts, brutalist aesthetics, and bespoke micro-interactions
- Direct links to live websites allowing direct inspection of DOM structures, custom CSS, and animation runtimes
- Daily curation surfacing emerging creative agencies, portfolio sites, and experimental web studios

## Stack Fit & Workflow

Essential creative inspiration for design technologists, creative frontend engineers, and art directors crafting bespoke, memorable web experiences.
