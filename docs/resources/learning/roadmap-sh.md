# roadmap.sh

## Why Use It

Keeping track of evolving skill requirements across software engineering disciplines is difficult when relying on disorganized blogs and fragmented forum posts. roadmap.sh is the community's leading interactive educational platform, providing visually organized, community-curated learning roadmaps for every developer career track. It empowers engineers to assess their competencies, track skill acquisition, and follow clear pedagogical pathways from junior to senior engineering roles.

## Key Capabilities

- Interactive visual roadmaps for Frontend, Backend, DevOps, Full Stack, Android, iOS, Blockchain, and AI Engineering
- Granular node-level documentation detailing why each technology matters, with links to canonical tutorials and videos
- Skill assessment quizzes and hands-on project suggestions tied to specific milestones in the roadmap
- Personalized progress tracking allowing developers to mark completed topics and share customized roadmaps

## Stack Fit & Workflow

The global benchmark career navigation tool for engineering teams, university students, bootcamp learners, and engineering managers structuring career progression frameworks.
