# Devpost

## Why Use It

Finding verified software hackathons with substantial sponsor prize pools often requires searching across scattered university and corporate websites. Devpost is the world's largest hackathon platform, hosting hundreds of virtual and in-person developer competitions backed by major tech enterprises (Google, Microsoft, AWS). It serves as both a global competition portal and an open portfolio showcase where developers document project architectures and project builds.

## Key Capabilities

- Global hackathon discovery engine filterable by technology stack, prize pool, format (in-person/online), and eligibility
- Comprehensive project portfolio showcase indexing project stories, architectural diagrams, GitHub repos, and video demos
- Structured submission management handling multi-track sponsor bounties, team rosters, and judging rubrics
- Extensive community archive of tens of thousands of past hackathon projects for architectural inspiration

## Stack Fit & Workflow

Indispensable competition and portfolio platform for software engineers, student developers, and hackathon competitors building full-stack, AI, and cloud prototypes.
