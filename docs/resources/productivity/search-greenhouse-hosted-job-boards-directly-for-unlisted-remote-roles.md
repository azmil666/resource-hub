# Search Greenhouse hosted job boards directly for unlisted remote roles

## Why Use It

Enterprise technology companies and scale-ups rely heavily on Greenhouse to manage their hiring pipelines, creating direct career boards at boards.greenhouse.io. Searching through public job aggregators often displays expired listings or buried openings. Using targeted Google search syntax enables engineers to query thousands of enterprise job boards directly, surfacing unindexed and freshly published remote roles.

## Key Capabilities

- Targeted Google search query syntax indexing raw company career boards hosted on Greenhouse
- Direct access to primary ATS job applications, eliminating recruiter intermediary barriers
- Customizable Boolean operators for targeting specific engineering levels (Staff, Principal, Senior) and tech stacks
- Early discovery of newly posted requisitions before they are picked up by automated job alert scrapers

## Stack Fit & Workflow

Essential search technique for software developers and engineering leaders seeking high-signal remote roles at top-tier tech companies.
