# Hack Club

## Why Use It

Young aspiring programmers often lack structured mentorship, hardware resources, and supportive peer communities to build real-world software and hardware projects. Hack Club is a global nonprofit organization that empowers teenage developers by supporting in-person coding clubs, providing micro-grants for hardware prototyping, and organizing free student coding events. It provides youth with an authentic, hacker-first culture focused on shipping real projects.

## Key Capabilities

- Global community platform connecting tens of thousands of student developers on an active, moderated Slack community
- Hardware grant programs (such as Sprig and OnBoard) funding custom printed circuit boards (PCBs) and microcontroller projects
- Open-source educational workshops guiding students through building games, web apps, and embedded systems
- Free nonprofit fiscal sponsorship (Hack Club Bank) for high school hackathons and student tech initiatives

## Stack Fit & Workflow

An essential community and resource incubator for youth programmers, student hackers, and high school computer science educators.
