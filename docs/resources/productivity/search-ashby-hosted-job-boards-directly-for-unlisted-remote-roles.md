# Search Ashby hosted job boards directly for unlisted remote roles

## Why Use It

High-growth tech startups and venture-backed companies increasingly use modern Applicant Tracking Systems like Ashby, often publishing high-paying remote roles on their direct job boards before syndicating them to noisy job aggregator sites. This Google search syntax allows job seekers to bypass saturated public job boards and index open positions directly from company Ashby portals. It surfaces unlisted, fresh remote job opportunities ahead of aggregator scraping delays.

## Key Capabilities

- Direct index querying targeting official Ashby company career portals (jobs.ashbyhq.com)
- Bypasses spammy job aggregator boards, outdated listings, and recruiter reposts
- Precise Boolean search filtering by exact job title, remote geography, and technical specialties
- Surfaces early-stage tech startup and unicorn roles that never pay for syndication on major job boards

## Stack Fit & Workflow

A high-leverage search methodology for software engineers, engineering managers, and technical contributors sourcing direct-to-company remote job openings.
