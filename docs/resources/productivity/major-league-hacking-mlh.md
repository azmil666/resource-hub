# Major League Hacking (MLH)

## Why Use It

Student hackathons require rigorous event logistics, developer hardware labs, industry mentorship, and fair judging standards to provide positive learning outcomes. Major League Hacking (MLH) is the official student hackathon league, supporting hundreds of accredited weekend hackathons and workshops serving over 100,000 developers annually. It provides students with hardware access, cloud credits, and the MLH Fellowship program for open-source software contributions.

## Key Capabilities

- Official accreditation and support for hundreds of university student hackathons across North America and Europe
- MLH Hardware Lab providing hackathon attendees with free access to VR headsets, microcontrollers, and IoT sensors
- MLH Fellowship program pairing student engineers with paid internships contributing to major open-source repositories
- Standardized code of conduct and judging guidelines ensuring inclusive, high-integrity hackathon competitions

## Stack Fit & Workflow

A cornerstone organization for university developers, open-source apprentices, and developer relations teams connecting with early-career talent.
